Clinical Rare Blood Notes

Bombay Blood (hh): Individuals lack the H antigen. They can donate red cells widely, but can only receive from another Bombay (hh) donor!

Rh-null ("Golden Blood"): Completely lacks Rh system antigens. Extremely rare worldwide; can only receive Rh-null blood.

⚠️ Pre-donation reminder: Informational red-cell guide only. Hospital laboratory crossmatch and pre-donation hemoglobin check (Hb > 12.5) are mandatory prior to transfusion.
